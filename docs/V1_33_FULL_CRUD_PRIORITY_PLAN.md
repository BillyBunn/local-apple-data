Contacts note gates remain designed and synthetic-tested but are live-unavailable on the current helper with `contacts_note_unavailable` because Apple's restricted notes entitlement is absent.

## Objective

Make full CRUD a first-class product priority for the local Apple data plugin, starting with the essential surfaces:

- Mail
- Messages
- Calendar
- Reminders
- Notes

Essential surfaces: Mail, Messages, Calendar, Reminders, and Notes.

Full CRUD does not mean ungated bulk mutation. In this plugin, CRUD means staged, exact-target create/read/update/delete capability where every mutating operation has preview, approval-token apply, independent read-back, synthetic tests, redaction coverage, installed-cache verification, and explicit approval before public exposure.
